# 🛍️ Customer Behaviour Analysis

End-to-end analysis of customer shopping behaviour using **Python**, **SQL** and **Power BI**: from cleaning raw data to answering business questions and building an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To understand how different customer groups spend, which products perform best, and how discounts, shipping type and subscriptions affect revenue, so that the business can make better pricing, marketing and product decisions.

## 🗂️ Dataset

- **Source:** [Customer_Shopping_Behaviour]
- **Size:** [3900] rows × [18] columns
- **Key columns:** [e.g. customer age, gender, item purchased, category, purchase amount, review rating, shipping type, discount applied, subscription status]

## 🛠️ Tools Used

| Stage | Tool |
|---|---|
| Data cleaning & EDA | Python (Pandas, NumPy) |
| Business analysis | SQL (PostgreSQL) |
| Dashboard | Power BI |

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & EDA (Python)
- Explored the dataset to understand its structure, data types and summary statistics
- Identified and **removed duplicate records**
- Checked for **missing values** and filled them with the **median**
- Applied **frequency mapping** to prepare categorical fields
- Loaded the cleaned data into a SQL database

### 2. Business Analysis (SQL)
Answered the following business questions with SQL queries:

1. What is the **total revenue generated by male and female customers**?
2. Which customers **used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount**?
3. Which are the **top 5 products with the highest average review rating**?
4. How does the **average purchase amount compare between Standard and Express shipping**?
5. Do **subscribed customers spend more**? Comparison of average spend and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers
6. Which **5 products have the highest percentage of purchases with a discount applied**?

### 3. Dashboard (Power BI)
Connected the SQL database to Power BI and built an interactive dashboard to present the key metrics and trends.

## 📈 Key Insights

**Revenue & Customers**
- Total revenue across **3,900 customers** was **233,081**, an average of roughly **59.77** per customer.
- **Male customers generated 157,890 (67.7%)** of revenue versus **75,191 (32.3%)** from female customers, so male customers contributed more than double.
- **Young Adults** were the highest-revenue age group at **62,143 (26.7%)**, about 11% more than Seniors (55,763), the lowest. Revenue across the four age groups is fairly evenly spread.

**Customer Loyalty**
- **79.9% of customers (3,116) are Loyal**, 18.0% (701) are Returning, and only **2.1% (83) are New**. The business relies heavily on its existing base, with very little new-customer acquisition.
- **Subscribers have a higher repeat-buyer rate**: 958 of 1,053 subscribers (91.0%) are repeat buyers versus 2,518 of 2,847 non-subscribers (88.4%).

**Subscriptions**
- Subscribers are **27% of customers** and bring in **26.9% of revenue (62,645)**. Their average spend (59.49) is almost identical to non-subscribers (59.87), so subscribing does **not** increase spend per purchase.

**Shipping & Discounts**
- **Express shipping customers spend more** per order (**60.48**) than Standard (**58.46**), about 3.5% higher.
- Discounts are applied most often on **Hat (50%)**, **Sneakers (49%)**, **Coat (49%)**, **Sweater (48%)** and **Pants (47%)**, so nearly half of purchases of these items involve a discount.
- [N] customers used a discount and **still spent above the average purchase amount**, showing that discounts don't always reduce order value.

**Products**
- Highest-rated products: **Gloves (3.86)**, **Sandals (3.84)**, **Boots (3.82)**, **Hat (3.80)** and **Skirt (3.78)**. The gap between first and fifth is small.
- Top sellers by orders: **Jewelry (171)** in Accessories, **Blouse and Pants (171 each)** in Clothing, **Sandals (160)** in Footwear and **Jacket (163)** in Outerwear.

**Business Takeaways**
- Invest in **new-customer acquisition**, since only 2.1% of customers are new.
- Review the **subscription benefits**, because subscribers don't spend more per order.
- Consider **promoting Express shipping**, since it is linked to higher order values.
